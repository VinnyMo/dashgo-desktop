Qt libraries are dynamically loaded from _internal/PySide6 and
_internal/shiboken6. Preserve compatible library/binding versions together.
Close the application, back up its program files and Data, replace the
compatible libraries, then launch DashGo.exe. No signature check prevents
replacement. Rebuild instructions and application source are available in

with an ABI-incompatible version is not supported. A complete modified-Qt
